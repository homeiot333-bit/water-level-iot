Quick Installation & User Guide

1. Very Simple Installation Guide

Follow these two simple steps to physically set up your device:

  1. Install the Rooftop Unit: Place the sensor box directly above your water tank. Make sure the two round "eyes" are pointing straight down at the water. Do not let the sensor touch the water.
  2. Plug in the Main Hub: Keep the Main Hub inside your house in a dry, safe place. Plug it into a normal wall socket and turn the power on.

2. First Time Setup & Getting Your Password

  1. Press and hold the button on the Main Hub for 5 seconds until the light starts blinking 🩷 PINK.
  2. Open your mobile phone, go to Wi-Fi settings, and connect to the network named "SmartWater_Setup".
  3. A setup page will open on your phone automatically. IMPORTANT: Write down the Login Email and Password shown on this screen! You will need them to log into the website.
  4. Select your home Wi-Fi name, enter your home Wi-Fi password, and click save. The machine will restart and connect to the internet automatically!

3. Light Colors (LEDs) - What do they mean?

  • 🟢 GREEN: Tank is 100% Full.
  • 🔵 BLUE: Tank is Below 75%.
  • 🟡 YELLOW: Tank is Below 50%.
  • 🔴 RED (Water LED): Tank is Below 25% (Pump will turn on automatically).
  • 🩷 PINK (Pump LED): The water pump is currently actively running.
  • 🔴 RED (Connection LED): Solid Red means the device is successfully connected to the internet.
  • 🩷 PINK (Blinking): The device is in Wi-Fi Setup Mode waiting for your phone.

4. How to Turn the Pump ON/OFF Manually

The system is fully automatic, but if you want to control it yourself:

  • From the Machine: Press the physical button on the Main Hub exactly one time.
  • From the Website: Login to the website and click the giant round button. It will show 🟢 GREEN when running, and 🔴 RED when stopped.

5. Website Features & Changing Your Password

Once you log into the web dashboard with your saved Email and Password, you can enjoy:

  • Live Animated Tank: Watch water fill up and drop in real-time.
  • Solar & Battery BMS: View live battery percentage, solar charging status, backup time, and cell health automatically when connected.
  • Daily Usage Tracker: See exactly how much water your house consumes every day to stop wastage.
  • Engineering Data: View live signal quality and exact distance measurements.
  • Change Password: Click the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner of the dashboard, and select "Change Password" to make your account secure.

6. Troubleshooting (Fault Lights)

Symptom / LED Status Meaning & Easy Fix
Connection LED is Blinking Red. The machine lost your home Wi-Fi signal. Check if your router is turned on. If your Wi-Fi password changed recently, do the 5-second button hold to set it up again.
Pump LED is ON, but water is not filling. The machine is sending power, but your pump has no water to pull. Turn the pump OFF from the website immediately so it does not burn out!
Red Light & Pink Light are Blinking together. Sensor Error. The Main Hub cannot talk to the Rooftop unit, or the sensor has water drops on it. Safely wipe the bottom of the sensor with a dry cloth.
Fast Blinking Red Light. OVERFLOW DANGER! Water is hitting the sensor. The pump has automatically locked itself to keep you safe.

7. Safety Precautions

  • Do NOT open the Main Hub. High voltage (220V AC) is present inside. Opening it is dangerous.
  • Protect from Direct Rain: Keep the Main Hub in a dry area protected from rain.
