Quick Installation & User Guide

1. Very Simple Installation Guide

Follow these two simple steps to physically set up your device:

  1. Install the Rooftop Unit: Place the sensor box directly above your water tank. Make sure the two round "eyes" are pointing straight down at the water. Do not let the sensor touch the water.
  2. Plug in the Main Hub: Keep the Main Hub inside your house in a dry, safe place. Plug it into a normal wall socket and turn the power on.

2. First Time Setup & Getting Your Password

  1. Press the Big Red Button on the Main Hub 3 times fast until the Pump Status light turns ON.
  2. Open your mobile phone, go to Wi-Fi settings, and connect to the network named "Water_Level_Setup".
  3. A setup page will open on your phone automatically. IMPORTANT: Write down the MAC ID, Login Email, and Password shown at the top of this screen! You will need them to log into the website.
  4. Enter your home Wi-Fi name and password, and click Save. The machine will restart and connect to the internet automatically!

3. Light Colors (LEDs) - What do they mean?

  • 🟢 GREEN (FULL): Tank is 75% to 100% Full.
  • 🔵 BLUE (HIGH): Tank is 50% to 74%.
  • 🟡 YELLOW (MEDIUM): Tank is 25% to 49%.
  • 🔴 RED (LOW - Solid): Tank is Below 25% (Pump will turn on automatically).
  • 🔴 RED (LOW - Blinking Every 1 Second): Dry-Run Protection! Your ground reservoir had no water for 2.5 minutes, so the pump automatically turned OFF to protect the motor.
  • 🩷 PUMP STATUS LED: Blinking when the water pump is actively running.
  • 🔴 CONNECTION LED: Blinking when there is a radio/sensor link fault.

4. How to Turn the Pump ON/OFF Manually

The system is fully automatic, but if you want to control it yourself:

  • From the Machine: Press and hold the physical red button on the Main Hub for 3 seconds.
  • From the Website: Login to the website and click the round Pump Control button.

5. Website Features & Changing Your Password

Once you log into the web dashboard with your saved Email and Password, you can enjoy:

  • Live Animated Tank: Watch water fill up and drop in real-time.
  • Solar & Battery BMS: View live battery percentage, solar charging status, backup time, and cell health automatically when connected.
  • Daily Usage Tracker: See exactly how much water your house consumes every day to stop wastage.
  • Engineering Data: View live signal quality and exact distance measurements.
  • Change Password: Click the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner of the dashboard, and select "Change Password" to make your account secure.

6. Troubleshooting (Fault Lights)

Symptom / LED Status Meaning & Easy Fix
LOW (25%) Red LED is Blinking Every 1 Second. Dry-Run Auto Cutoff: The pump ran for 2.5 minutes, but water did not enter the tank (ground reservoir is empty). Refill your ground reservoir, then turn the pump ON manually.
Connection LED & Pump Status LED Blinking together. Sensor Error. The Main Hub cannot talk to the Rooftop unit, or the sensor has water drops on it. Safely wipe the bottom of the sensor with a dry cloth.
Fast Blinking FULL Green Light. OVERFLOW DANGER! Water is hitting the sensor. The pump has automatically locked itself to keep you safe.

7. Safety Precautions

  • Do NOT open the Main Hub. High voltage (220V AC) is present inside. Opening it is dangerous.
  • Protect from Direct Rain: Keep the Main Hub in a dry area protected from rain.
